1 Analytics Investigation 1: Descriptive Analytics

1.1 1. Analytics Type Selected

Descriptive Analytics: Understanding “what is happening” within the subscriber base before attempting to diagnose causes, predict future attrition, or prescribe retention interventions.

1.2 2. Framed Business Question

“What is the churn rate and average revenue per user (ARPU) across different customer segments (e.g., by customer value tier, usage level, recharge behavior, and tenure), and how does ARPU differ between customers who churn and those who don’t, for an Indian telecom operator?”

1.3 3. Datasets Required

Dataset Purpose Level Period Covered
Telecom Churn Case Study (Kaggle) Micro customer-level records including monthly ARPU, incoming/outgoing minutes of usage (MOU), recharge frequency & amounts, 2G/3G data volumes, and churn labels. Micro (Customer-level) June 2014 – September 2014 (Months 6 to 9)
TRAI Performance Indicator Reports (2014 & Modern) Macro regulatory reports from TRAI providing nationwide prepaid/postpaid ARPU, wireless subscriber totals, and MOU benchmarks. Macro (Market-level) Q2 2014 (QE Sep-14) & Q4 2025 (QE Mar-25)

1.4 4. Access Plan & Provenance Logging

All data acquisition is completely reproducible via automated scripts and official repositories:

  1. Customer-Level Dataset: Acquired via Kaggle API from the competition telecom-churn-case-study-hackathon-gc1 into data/raw/telecom-churn/train.csv.
  2. TRAI Regulatory Reports: Downloaded directly from the official portal of the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) at https://www.trai.gov.in/release-publication/reports/performance-indicators-reports.

3.3 Churn Definition

In Indian telecom business modeling, churn is behavioral: - Good Phase (Months 6 & 7 — June & July 2014): Subscriber behaves normally. - Action Phase (Month 8 — August 2014): Subscriber shows early signals of decay (e.g. usage and recharge drops). - Churn Phase (Month 9 — September 2014): A customer who makes zero incoming calls, zero outgoing calls, and consumes zero mobile data in Month 9 is defined as churned (churn_probability = 1).

3.4 High-Value Customer Filtering

To focus analytical and retention efforts where business value is concentrated, Indian telecom operators identify High-Value Customers (HVC). - Definition: Customers whose average total recharge amount in the Good Phase (Months 6 & 7) is greater than or equal to the 70th percentile.

# Compute combined recharge amount (calls + data recharge packs) for months 6 & 7
rech_amt_6 = df_raw['total_rech_amt_6'] + df_raw['total_rech_data_6'].fillna(0) * df_raw['av_rech_amt_data_6'].fillna(0)

df_clean = df_raw.copy()

# Add high-value indicator and average recharge
df_clean['avg_rech_amt_6_7'] = avg_rech_6_7
df_clean['is_high_value'] = is_high_value

# Month-over-month ARPU change features
df_clean['arpu_diff_7_6'] = df_clean['arpu_7'] - df_clean['arpu_6']
df_clean['arpu_diff_8_7'] = df_clean['arpu_8'] - df_clean['arpu_7']
df_clean['avg_arpu_6_7'] = (df_clean['arpu_6'] + df_clean['arpu_7']) / 2.0

# Usage and recharge activity differentials
df_clean['total_og_mou_diff_8_7'] = df_clean['total_og_mou_8'] - df_clean['total_og_mou_7']
df_clean['total_ic_mou_diff_8_7'] = df_clean['total_ic_mou_8'] - df_clean['total_ic_mou_7']
df_clean['total_rech_num_diff_8_7'] = df_clean['total_rech_num_8'] - df_clean['total_rech_num_7']
df_clean['total_rech_amt_diff_8_7'] = df_clean['total_rech_amt_8'] - df_clean['total_rech_amt_7']

# Tenure segmentation from aon (age on network in days -> months)
df_clean['tenure_months'] = (df_clean['aon'] / 30.44).astype(int)
df_clean['tenure_group'] = pd.cut(
    df_clean['tenure_months'],
    bins=[-1, 12, 24, 48, 9999],
    labels=['0-1 yr', '1-2 yrs', '2-4 yrs', '4+ yrs']
)

# Recharge frequency buckets
df_clean['rech_freq_bucket_6'] = pd.qcut(df_clean['total_rech_num_6'], q=3, labels=['Low', 'Medium', 'High'])

# Mobile data plan adoption
df_clean['has_data_plan'] = ((df_clean['total_rech_data_6'].fillna(0) > 0) | (df_clean['total_rech_data_7'].fillna(0) > 0)).astype(int)
df_clean['data_user_label'] = df_clean['has_data_plan'].map({1: 'Data User (2G/3G)', 0: 'Voice/SMS Only'})

# Human-readable churn label
df_clean['churn_label'] = df_clean['churn_probability'].map({0: 'Retained', 1: 'Churned'})

# Save cleaned dataset
df_clean.to_csv("data/processed/churn_clean.csv", index=False)

4.5.1 Analytical Note on the Reliance Jio Structural Break

CRITICAL MARKET BREAKDOWN (September 2016):

Direct comparisons between 2014 and 2025 ARPU figures must account for the structural transformation triggered by Reliance Jio’s market entry in September 2016:

  1. Pricing Paradigm Shift: In 2014, mobile operators operated on separate metered billing for voice calls (~50p/min) and expensive 2G/3G data packs (~₹250 per GB). Modern 2025 plans bundle unlimited nationwide voice calls with 1.5–2.0 GB/day of 4G/5G data for fixed monthly commitments (~₹200–₹350).
  2. Voice MOU Inflation: Because calls became completely unmetered over VoLTE/5G networks, national minutes of usage per subscriber expanded by +173%, rising from 376 minutes/month in 2014 to 1,026 minutes/month in 2025.
  3. Market Consolidation: Over 10 active operators competed in 2014 (Airtel, Vodafone, Idea, BSNL, Reliance Communications, Aircel, Tata Docomo, Telenor), whereas the industry has consolidated into a private duopoly/oligopoly (Jio, Airtel, Vi) alongside public operator BSNL.

## Churn Definition

In Indian telecom business modeling, churn is **behavioral**:
- **Good Phase (Months 6 & 7 — June & July 2014):** Subscriber behaves normally.
- **Action Phase (Month 8 — August 2014):** Subscriber shows early signals of decay (e.g. usage and recharge drops).
- **Churn Phase (Month 9 — September 2014):** A customer who makes **zero incoming calls, zero outgoing calls, and consumes zero mobile data** in Month 9 is defined as churned (`churn_probability = 1`).

## High-Value Customer Filtering

To focus analytical and retention efforts where business value is concentrated, Indian telecom operators identify **High-Value Customers (HVC)**.
- **Definition:** Customers whose average total recharge amount in the Good Phase (Months 6 & 7) is greater than or equal to the **70th percentile**.

```{python}
#| label: high-value-filtering-logic
#| echo: true
# Compute combined recharge amount (calls + data recharge packs) for months 6 & 7

ax.plot(months, ret_pts, marker="o", linewidth=2.5, markersize=8, color=STATUS_PALETTE[0], label="Retained Subscribers")
ax.plot(months, chu_pts, marker="s", linewidth=2.5, markersize=8, color=STATUS_PALETTE[1], label="Churned Subscribers")

for i, (r_val, c_val) in enumerate(zip(ret_pts, chu_pts)):
    ax.annotate(f"₹{r_val:.1f}", (i, r_val), textcoords="offset points", xytext=(0, 10), ha="center", weight="bold", color=STATUS_PALETTE[0])
    ax.annotate(f"₹{c_val:.1f}", (i, c_val), textcoords="offset points", xytext=(0, -18), ha="center", weight="bold", color=STATUS_PALETTE[1])

ax.set_title("Monthly ARPU Trajectory Leading Up to Churn", fontsize=14, fontweight="bold", pad=15)
ax.set_ylabel("Mean Monthly ARPU (₹)")
ax.set_ylim(50, 360)
ax.legend(frameon=True, loc="upper right")
plt.tight_layout()
plt.show()
```

> **Key Behavioral Finding:** In the "Good Phase" (Month 6), churners actually generated **₹308.29 mean ARPU**, higher than retained subscribers (₹280.28). However, during the "Action Phase" (Month 8), churner ARPU plummeted by **-62.95% to ₹114.23**, providing a strong leading diagnostic signal prior to complete churn in Month 9.

### Analytical Note on the Reliance Jio Structural Break

> **CRITICAL MARKET BREAKDOWN (September 2016):**
>
> Direct comparisons between 2014 and 2025 ARPU figures must account for the structural transformation triggered by **Reliance Jio's market entry in September 2016**:
>
> 1. **Pricing Paradigm Shift:** In 2014, mobile operators operated on separate metered billing for voice calls (~50p/min) and expensive 2G/3G data packs (~₹250 per GB). Modern 2025 plans bundle unlimited nationwide voice calls with 1.5–2.0 GB/day of 4G/5G data for fixed monthly commitments (~₹200–₹350).
> 2. **Voice MOU Inflation:** Because calls became completely unmetered over VoLTE/5G networks, national minutes of usage per subscriber expanded by **+173%**, rising from **376 minutes/month in 2014** to **1,026 minutes/month in 2025**.
> 3. **Market Consolidation:** Over 10 active operators competed in 2014 (Airtel, Vodafone, Idea, BSNL, Reliance Communications, Aircel, Tata Docomo, Telenor), whereas the industry has consolidated into a private duopoly/oligopoly (Jio, Airtel, Vi) alongside public operator BSNL.
