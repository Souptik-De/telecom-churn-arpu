1 Analytics Investigation 1: Descriptive Analytics

1.1 1. Analytics Type Selected

Descriptive Analytics: Understanding “what is happening” within the subscriber base before attempting to diagnose causes, predict future attrition, or prescribe retention interventions.

1.2 2. Framed Business Question

“What is the churn rate and average revenue per user (ARPU) across different customer segments (e.g., by customer value tier, usage level, recharge behavior, and tenure), and how does ARPU differ between customers who churn and those who don’t, for an Indian telecom operator?”

3.3 Behavioral Churn Definition

In Indian telecom business modeling, churn is behavioral: - Good Phase (Months 6 & 7 — June & July 2014): Subscriber behaves normally. - Action Phase (Month 8 — August 2014): Subscriber shows early signals of decay (usage and recharge drops). - Churn Phase (Month 9 — September 2014): A customer who makes zero incoming calls, zero outgoing calls, and consumes zero mobile data in Month 9 is defined as churned (churn_probability = 1).

3.4 High-Value Customer (HVC) Filtering

To focus analytical and retention efforts where business revenue is concentrated, Indian telecom operators isolate High-Value Customers (HVC). - Definition: Customers whose average total recharge amount in the Good Phase (Months 6 & 7) is \(\ge\) 70th percentile.

# Tenure segmentation from aon (age on network in days -> months)
df_clean['tenure_months'] = (df_clean['aon'] / 30.44).astype(int)
df_clean['tenure_group'] = pd.cut(
    df_clean['tenure_months'],
    bins=[-1, 12, 24, 48, 9999],
    labels=['0-1 yr', '1-2 yrs', '2-4 yrs', '4+ yrs']
)

# Recharge frequency buckets
df_clean['rech_freq_bucket_6'] = pd.qcut(df_clean['total_rech_num_6'], q=3, labels=['Low', 'Medium', 'High'])

# Mobile data plan adoption

4.5.1 Analytical Note on the Reliance Jio Structural Break

CRITICAL MARKET BREAKDOWN (September 2016):

Direct comparisons between 2014 and 2025 ARPU figures must account for the structural transformation triggered by Reliance Jio’s market entry in September 2016:

  1. Pricing Paradigm Shift: In 2014, mobile operators operated on separate metered billing for voice calls (~50p/min) and expensive 2G/3G data packs (~₹250 per GB). Modern 2025 plans bundle unlimited nationwide voice calls with 1.5–2.0 GB/day of 4G/5G data for fixed monthly commitments (~₹200–₹350).
  2. Voice MOU Inflation: Because calls became completely unmetered over VoLTE/5G networks, national minutes of usage per subscriber expanded by +173%, rising from 376 minutes/month in 2014 to 1,026 minutes/month in 2025.
  3. Market Consolidation: Over 10 active operators competed in 2014 (Airtel, Vodafone, Idea, BSNL, Reliance Communications, Aircel, Tata Docomo, Telenor), whereas the industry has consolidated into a private duopoly/oligopoly (Jio, Airtel, Vi) alongside public operator BSNL.

## Behavioral Churn Definition

In Indian telecom business modeling, churn is **behavioral**:
- **Good Phase (Months 6 & 7 — June & July 2014):** Subscriber behaves normally.
- **Action Phase (Month 8 — August 2014):** Subscriber shows early signals of decay (usage and recharge drops).
- **Churn Phase (Month 9 — September 2014):** A customer who makes **zero incoming calls, zero outgoing calls, and consumes zero mobile data** in Month 9 is defined as churned (`churn_probability = 1`).

## High-Value Customer (HVC) Filtering

To focus analytical and retention efforts where business revenue is concentrated, Indian telecom operators isolate **High-Value Customers (HVC)**.
- **Definition:** Customers whose average total recharge amount in the Good Phase (Months 6 & 7) is $\ge$ **70th percentile**.

### Analytical Note on the Reliance Jio Structural Break

> **CRITICAL MARKET BREAKDOWN (September 2016):**
>
> Direct comparisons between 2014 and 2025 ARPU figures must account for the structural transformation triggered by **Reliance Jio's market entry in September 2016**:
>
> 1. **Pricing Paradigm Shift:** In 2014, mobile operators operated on separate metered billing for voice calls (~50p/min) and expensive 2G/3G data packs (~₹250 per GB). Modern 2025 plans bundle unlimited nationwide voice calls with 1.5–2.0 GB/day of 4G/5G data for fixed monthly commitments (~₹200–₹350).
> 2. **Voice MOU Inflation:** Because calls became completely unmetered over VoLTE/5G networks, national minutes of usage per subscriber expanded by **+173%**, rising from **376 minutes/month in 2014** to **1,026 minutes/month in 2025**.
> 3. **Market Consolidation:** Over 10 active operators competed in 2014 (Airtel, Vodafone, Idea, BSNL, Reliance Communications, Aircel, Tata Docomo, Telenor), whereas the industry has consolidated into a private duopoly/oligopoly (Jio, Airtel, Vi) alongside public operator BSNL.
